As an overlander, I want to download high-resolution topographic maps for offline use, so that I can navigate safely when I lose cellular service in remote wilderness areas. 

As a rock crawler, I want to filter trails by minimum required tire size and differential locker recommendations, so that I avoid wasting time driving to a trailhead that my current rig cannot handle. 

As a mud bogger, I want to see real-time weather overlays and recent rainfall totals for a trail area, so that I can identify which trails currently have the deepest and most challenging mud pits. 

As a pavement princess, I want to find mapped waypoints for highly photogenic scenic overlooks with easy parking, so that I can take great golden-hour photos of my rig for social media.
